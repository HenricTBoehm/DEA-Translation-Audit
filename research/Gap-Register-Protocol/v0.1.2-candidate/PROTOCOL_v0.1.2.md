## Question and bounds

What is the smallest practically relevant decision situation that an established governance and evidence procedure cannot adjudicate adequately, and can DEA provide a demonstrable additional capability there? An established procedure or composition may meet every studied duty. DEA may have a scoped advantage, fail a duty, or remain unevaluable. Equality on this test domain is operational evidence within the defined profile. Equivalence of entire architectures would need a separate argument.

Study one sealed score, one binary decision, one version-bound evaluation policy and a bounded provenance trace. The primary scientific control check concerns the integrity of the criterion actually used. Fix the remaining authorization/disposition gates to pass. RELEASE in this study denotes passage of this control gate. Numerical eligibility derived from the score is a separate result. No safety conclusion is inferred about a release in a complete real system.

Use D0, the original DEA v0.3.5.1 manuscript, separately from D1, D0 amended by nonnormative P-01. Read the exact historical sources in [SOURCE_MANIFEST.md](SOURCE_MANIFEST.md). The no-independent-advantage result for Decision Stability versus ordinary joint sensitivity analysis under equal evidence from PR #11 stays historical context; it is outside the new hypotheses. General detection of completely omitted dependencies from identical evidence is impossible. The extent of functional overlap between complete governance systems remains open.

## Execution order and stop conditions

1. Verify exact source bytes and independently approve the chosen harm, neutral grammar, oracles and prospectively fixed thresholds. Independently approve evidence mapping, issuer assumptions, tool build hashes and resource limits.
2. Assign an assessor/custodian independent of participants. Freeze the public protocol, the allowable generator and custody plan. Have the custodian obtain and validate actual holdout inputs privately before participant implementation and record the custody milestone. Include public hashes only where they do not expose private low-entropy data.
3. A named authority records date, exact commits, artifact fingerprints and independent acceptance. Only then begin a separate implementation task. Freeze participant executables before revealing any held-out requests. Capture S3 and S4 evidence during execution.
4. Report Stratum A before Stratum B as separate experiments. Archive raw measurements securely and release evidence sufficient for authorized reproduction. Any rule change after examining participant outputs needs a prospective separate amendment and fresh hidden cases. Retain unfavorable or inconclusive results.

Stop if original sources cannot be verified, a premise lacks an accountable issuer, isolation or equal access is unverified, runtime/profile budgets are unequal, or test design changes after outputs are seen. Mark blocked experiments NOT EXECUTED and a question without sufficient evidence UNDETERMINED. A missing implementation or unsupported mapping is an observed limitation of that profile, with a separate expressibility proof burden.
