# Baselines and measurements

The three arms share the same attempt records and declared scientific scope. C invokes the pinned P-01/HR-1 governance component. C+S attaches ordinary exact joint sensitivity. D attaches the unchanged PR #10 diagnostic. C+S and D share the scope adapter and both receive claimed finite coverage where supplied. The independent C+S implementation enumerates observed worlds and finds a minimum query by hitting the symmetric differences between opposing-output pairs. D partitions the world rows by retained facts. For an exact finite table, both tests accept a query exactly when each opposite-output pair differs on a queried coordinate. Global joint variation supplies the interaction information that a one-at-a-time test can miss. [Saltelli et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envsoft.2019.01.012)

**Comparison result.** Identical scientific status, possible values and minimum query in all 16 named comparisons (12 synthetic records and four variants of two NIST benchmarks), and in 324 additional exhaustive small-grid configurations, including samples and empty domains. The governance bytes agreed across all three arms on all 16 named records. Ordinary sensitivity with the same certified table can make the same formal statement. Knowledge that the table covers the real domain comes from evidence outside either sensitivity algorithm. DEA-specific governance and exact version binding are already in C and are shared by C+S and D. They matter for which question is authorized and which attempt is judged.

Unit of relevance is one factor per case: the independent exact reference contains two admissible situations differing in that factor alone and with different outputs. Count the selected sufficient questions separately from presence in the observed schema. Three of 12 relevant occurrences are wholly absent. Other misses involve wrong effects, omitted worlds, unavailable certificates or a sampled scope. A false stable claim is a declared CERTIFIED_STABLE with multiple examiner-reference outcomes; a false completeness claim is a completed nonempty diagnostic under a positive coverage flag and a reference deficit (factor, effect, world or mandatory binding). The checker detects supplied reference omissions and mismatches, independently of whether the omitted aspect changes output in the tested fixture. Unknown scopes and abstentions are excluded from claim denominators. C has no scientific diagnostic in this comparison. In Q-C the shared governance model cleared a true conflict because the mandatory binding was omitted. The public benchmark variants supply no measured governance ground truth.

## Is DEA already available under another name?

| Established method | Overlap with the tested DEA mechanism | Limit of this comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Global sensitivity and exact finite scenario variation | Evaluates the same outcomes under the same declared uncertain inputs. The joint enumeration plus a sufficient-question calculation exactly matched D here. | The experiment concerns finite, fixed procedures. It supplies no new method for finding absent dimensions. |
| Requirements validation, traceability and independent model V&V | Compare the procedure, assumptions and dependency inventory to independent requirements and evidence. This is the work that detected hidden flags in Norris and Pontius. NASA-STD-7009B also separates model credibility from the program authority approving acceptance criteria. | No institutional process was observed. Performance relative to performing these practices in production is unresolved. |
| Workflow integrity and separation of duty; signed artifact provenance | Clark–Wilson is an integrity and role-separation antecedent. in-toto binds expected steps, authorized signers and artifacts to signed evidence. PROV provides a vocabulary for tracing derivations and influence. | Authorization of named signers or existence of a provenance graph does not by itself supply a certified upper bound on effective historical control. The specific translation of P-01/HR-1 witnesses, residuals and holds into existing policy mechanisms was not run. |

**Result for the equivalence hypothesis.** The diagnostic tested here has an existing counterpart: exact joint sensitivity under a warranted scope, plus the ordinary calculation of sufficient observations. The omission audit matched requirements and model validation steps. A practical combination of established methods reproduces these functions within the tested scope. The hypothesis that a particular existing named system is extensionally equivalent to the whole DEA governance architecture is UNDETERMINED. A valid test of that stronger claim would compare authorization, negative coverage obligations and historical control on the same evidence. For example Q-G distinguishes signer-role separation alone from an effective common controller over the producer and mandatory rubric. A signed provenance system extended with equivalent control policy may still handle that case. The present results justify no novelty claim for Decision Stability and no conclusion that the whole DEA system already exists under one name.
