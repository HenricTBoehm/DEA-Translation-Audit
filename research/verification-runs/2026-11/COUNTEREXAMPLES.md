## Proof and scope

For nonempty windows, A paths become C journeys by choosing a single shared time. Every C journey is a B path after projection. The forward and reverse relays show strictness. With an empty window the implementation's common reflexive convention gives the same inclusion. Monotonicity for A, B and C at fixed windows follows by witness preservation under edge addition. In a nonempty static window every static path admits a same-instant assignment, so all three relations coincide. Static SCC members can concatenate paths through one another to reach the same targets. Locality of each causal row to its own window proves independence from later edges for that row. ADR's sampled I4 check perturbs edges after the maximum of the two bounds and has a narrower test scope than this row-wise argument.

For C without resets, a journey can lose a repeated-vertex loop and retain nondecreasing timestamps. This justifies the simple-route enumeration independently of the original forward closure. For C-R the independent check uses a time-expanded graph and explicitly models its chosen reset convention. Its CM8 result depends on that convention. A-PLANT, record completeness, authentic edge scope and chronological tie-breaking are assumptions or open specifications. An invariant over a finite test space cannot establish that its model corresponds to real authority or effects.

## Additional RP and VP boundaries

- **PROOF_GAP (VP R5, N4).** The static import guard and the 44 agreeing regression rows have bounded scope. Six expected rows were authored in the same audit session, including two revised after an initial run. This reduces independence of the confirmation. The target engines were also reviewed in VP E1–E6, with mixed results.
- **CONFIRMED (VP C1–C3).** 72 of 16,807 RP CTRL cases distinguish pointwise from union closure. The enumerated RP CTRL edge slots form a DAG, so those results give no cycle coverage. VP C4 reports 3,053 of 3,299 general samples produce cyclic role hierarchy images; output agreement is insufficient for a legal image.
- **SPECIFICATION_AMBIGUITY (VP D5).** Let `x=(content1,L1)`, `y=(content1,L2)`, and `z=(content2,L2)`. The harness's transitive merge puts all three in one case without a direct content, locator or lineage match between x and z. DEA v0.3.4.3 does not fix precedence among these relations. The scope of blocked reuse can depend on the choice.
