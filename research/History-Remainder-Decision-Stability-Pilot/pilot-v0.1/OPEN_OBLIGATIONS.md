# Open obligations

- Authenticate the actual initial pre-state separately from the upper bound. Establish channel, alias and backward-cone coverage, independence of issuers and reset scope in real systems. The tested hold on unknown coverage cannot detect a false completeness attestation.
- Prove soundness of the abstraction and transfers for the system under study. The proof sketch uses monotone, ordered, fact-scoped transfers and fixed deletions. A state-dependent reset can violate either inclusion.
- Validate a complete nonempty substantive world domain against a real executable procedure, including dynamic resolution and authoritative version binding. The finite fixture performs exact enumeration. It contains no general abstract domain or proof that arbitrary world samples cover reality.
- Define how calibration or another substantive historical fact can be observed and whether a smaller question is actually cheaper. Compare blind HR-1 alone, HR-1 plus ordinary sensitivity analysis, and the proposed packaged diagnostic using real decisions, timed attestations and controlled retrieval.
- Verify atomic evaluation and commit against the real version store. The pilot compares fixture version bindings and reports a gate. It does not make an effective scientific disposition.
- Scale the query search and assess whether the cost of world coverage outweighs the gains. Pilot fact counts are logical units, not measured organizational cost. No actual Poblete control history is inferred.
